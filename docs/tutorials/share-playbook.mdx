# Share a Playbook

Completed playbooks can be shared via a public link that anyone can access without authentication. This is useful for sharing demo configurations with colleagues or prospects.

## How to Share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Playbook">
    Navigate to the playbook you want to share from the Dashboard, or open it directly if you just compiled it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the Share Link">
    In the Playbook Viewer, click the **Share** button in the top toolbar. The share URL is copied to your clipboard automatically.

    The URL follows the format:
    ```
    https://segment-demo-builder.vercel.app/share/{playbook_id}
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the Link">
    Share the URL with anyone — no account or login is required to view it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Recipients See

The share page displays a **read-only** version of the playbook:

- All prompt steps with titles and prompt text
- The SE Demo Script tab
- Ability to download the demo script as Markdown

## What Recipients Don't See

- **Real credentials** — The share page shows the sanitized version with `YOUR_*` placeholders
- **Rehydration modal** — There's no way to inject credentials on the share page
- **Mark Complete** buttons — Progress tracking is only available to the playbook owner
- **Edit or delete** controls

<Info>
  The share page works because of a specific RLS policy that allows anonymous `SELECT` access to playbooks with `status = 'completed'`. Draft playbooks cannot be accessed via share links.
</Info>

## Analytics on Share Pages

When someone views a shared playbook, two anonymous events are tracked:

- **Shared Playbook Viewed** — with the `playbook_id` and `industry`
- **Shared Demo Script Downloaded** — if they download the demo script
