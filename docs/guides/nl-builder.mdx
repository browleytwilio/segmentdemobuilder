The Natural Language Builder is an alternative to the step-by-step wizard. Describe what you need in a sentence or two, and AI will extract the customer name, industry, persona, architecture flags, and suggested scenarios.

## How to Use

1. Navigate to **New Playbook** from the navbar
2. Click the **Describe with AI** tab
3. Type a description of your demo needs, for example:

> I need a fintech demo for a CTO focused on PII compliance and risk scoring. The customer is Acme Bank.

4. Click **Configure Playbook**
5. The AI parses your description and pre-fills the wizard
6. You're switched to the **Step-by-Step** wizard at Step 3 (Scenarios) with context already configured

## What Gets Extracted

| Field | Example Input | Extracted Value |
|-------|--------------|-----------------|
| Customer name | "The customer is Acme Bank" | Acme Bank |
| Industry | "fintech demo" | FinTech |
| Persona | "for a CTO" | CTO / Engineering |
| Architecture | "PII compliance" | `enableProfileAPI: true` |
| Scenarios | "risk scoring" | Suggested scenario slugs |

## Tips for Best Results

<Tip>
Be specific about the industry and persona. The AI maps these to the fixed set of options in the builder (E-commerce, B2B SaaS, FinTech, Media & Entertainment; CMO, CTO, Product Manager, Data Team).
</Tip>

- Mention the **customer name** explicitly
- Name the **industry** or describe it clearly
- Specify the **role** of the person you're demoing to
- Describe the **use cases** or features you want to highlight

## After Parsing

The builder switches to the wizard view at Step 3 with:
- **Step 1** (Context) pre-filled with customer name, industry, and persona
- **Step 2** (Architecture) pre-filled with relevant architecture flags
- **Step 3** (Scenarios) ready for you to review and adjust

You can still edit any field before proceeding to credentials and compilation.

## Error Handling

If the AI can't parse your description (too vague, missing industry, etc.), you'll see an error message suggesting you be more specific. You can also click **Use step-by-step wizard instead** to switch to the manual flow.
