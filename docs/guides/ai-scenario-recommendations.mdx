Smart Recommendations help you choose the most impactful demo scenarios for your prospect. Instead of manually scanning the scenario list, click **AI Suggest** and get curated recommendations with reasoning.

## How to Use

1. In the **Builder Wizard**, reach **Step 3: Scenarios**
2. Click the **AI Suggest** button at the top of the scenario list
3. Review the recommendations — each includes:
   - **Scenario name** and slug
   - **Reasoning** explaining why this scenario fits the prospect
   - **Impact score** (1-10) indicating expected demo impact
4. Click a recommendation to auto-select that scenario

## What the AI Considers

The recommendation engine receives:

- **Customer name** — for context-aware suggestions
- **Industry** — maps to industry-specific scenario catalogs
- **Persona** — adapts recommendations to what matters to CMOs vs. CTOs vs. Product Managers
- **Architecture flags** — only recommends scenarios compatible with enabled features

## Example

For a **FinTech CTO** with Profile API enabled, the AI might recommend:

| Scenario | Reasoning | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Real-time risk scoring | CTOs care about technical architecture; Profile API enables real-time trait lookups for risk models | 9/10 |
| PII compliance workflows | FinTech regulation is a top-of-mind concern; demonstrates Protocols governance | 8/10 |
| Cross-device identity | Shows Identity Resolution solving a concrete FinTech challenge (multi-app banking) | 7/10 |

## After Recommendations

Recommendations are additive — they auto-check scenarios but don't uncheck your existing selections. You can freely adjust after accepting recommendations.

<Info>
Recommendations use the `fast` model tier for quick responses. Typical response time is 2-4 seconds.
</Info>
